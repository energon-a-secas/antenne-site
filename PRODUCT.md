# Antenne: product one-pager

**What it is:** Fleet launches, features, and updates as a news feed

**Who it is for:** The fleet's own visitors, its operator, and the few people the
operator trusts to review. A visitor lands on any neorgon.com site and has no way
to know what shipped this week; the operator ships constantly and has no channel
that announces it without hand-writing HTML.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A corner popup on the hub catches you between tools, late evening
on a desktop: three fresh headlines, one click to the full story, one click to
dismiss. The desk half happens earlier, from any browser: a reviewer signs in
with their Neorgon account, reads the drafts the newsroom submitted that
morning, fixes a headline, spikes the weak ones and approves the rest, and
closes the tab. Reviewing needs no terminal and no git.

**What done looks like:** A visitor closes the popup knowing what changed this
week. On the other side, an approved story reaches the feed, the RSS and the hub
popup by itself a few minutes after approval, through one committed JSON file,
and the desk shows it committed, then live.
